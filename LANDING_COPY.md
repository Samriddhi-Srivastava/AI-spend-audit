## FAQ

**Is this free?**
Yes, the audit itself is free and runs in your browser. We don't charge you for it. We make money by referring users whose stacks have significant savings to Credex, which sources discounted AI credits. Most users will never need that — the audit on its own is the point.

**Do you store my pricing information?**
We store the audit (tools, plans, spend, savings) only if you submit your email for a shareable link. Without that step nothing leaves your browser except an anonymized call to our summary API.

**How accurate is the audit?**
The dollar figures come from a hardcoded pricing table that's verified against vendor pricing pages. Last verification date is shown on every audit. The recommendations are rule-based — they're consistent for the same input, not LLM guesses. The summary at the end uses Claude to phrase things, but every number is deterministic.

**My plan isn't in your dropdown. What now?**
Drop a note via the feedback link at the bottom. The pricing table is open and easy to extend — common requests get added within a day or two. Right now we cover ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot, Gemini, Cursor, Windsurf, OpenAI API, and Anthropic API.

**Why should I trust your recommendations over my own judgment?**
You shouldn't, fully. The audit surfaces things that are easy to miss — overlapping subscriptions, plan tiers that don't match team size, tools used for the wrong job. Treat it as a checklist, not a mandate. The shareable URL is designed so you can hand the result to a teammate and discuss it, not so you can act on it blindly.
