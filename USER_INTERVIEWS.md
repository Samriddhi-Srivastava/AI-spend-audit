## Status

User interviews are currently in progress.

The goal is to conduct 3 short conversations with:
- A developer using multiple AI coding tools
- A student or content creator using ChatGPT/Claude
- A small team member using AI tools collaboratively

The interview structure and questions have already been prepared, and findings will be added after conversations are completed.

Planned focus areas:
- AI subscription overlap
- Team vs individual plan inefficiencies
- Workflow-specific tool preferences
- Perceived value vs monthly spend
- Interest in automated AI spend optimization

---

## Planned Questions

1. Which AI tools are you currently paying for?
2. Approximately how much do you spend monthly?
3. Why did you choose your current stack?
4. Which tool feels most valuable?
5. Which subscription feels unnecessary?
6. Would an AI spend audit tool be useful for your workflow?
7. What would make you share this tool with others?

---

## Expected Outcomes

The interviews are intended to validate:
- Whether users are aware of AI subscription overlap
- Whether users compare plans before subscribing
- Whether teams struggle with AI cost visibility
- Whether actionable recommendations increase perceived value
